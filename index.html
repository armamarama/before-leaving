Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Who we are

Before Leaving is a mobile application developed by ArmaMarama. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

What data we collect

Before Leaving collects the following data, all of which stays on your device:

  • Location — Used to detect when you leave a saved place (geofence exit or GPS reading). Location is accessed only briefly during a departure event and is never stored beyond the detection moment.
  • Physical activity — Used as a supplementary signal to improve departure confidence scoring (e.g. walking vs. still). Never stored persistently.
  • Saved places and item lists — Stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Never uploaded.
  • Reminder history — A log of past departure reminders (place name, items, timestamp). Stored locally, max 20 entries.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifier.
  • We do not track your route, travel history, or movement patterns.
  • We do not share any data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.
  • We do not send any data to external servers. The app has no backend.

Background location

The app requests "Allow all the time" location permission so that departure reminders can fire even when the app is closed. This permission is used exclusively for geofence exit detection. Location is accessed for a few seconds per departure event — never continuously tracked.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The updated version will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
