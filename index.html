Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Remigo is a mobile application developed by ArmaMarama. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

What data we collect

All data collected by Remigo stays exclusively on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.

  • Location — Used to detect when you leave a saved place (geofence exit or GPS reading). Accessed only briefly during a departure event; never stored beyond the detection moment.
  • Physical activity — Used as a supplementary signal to improve departure confidence scoring (e.g. walking vs. still). Never stored persistently.
  • Saved places and item lists — Stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Never uploaded.
  • Reminder history — A log of past departure reminders (place name, items, timestamp). Stored locally, maximum 20 entries.

What we do NOT collect

✕ Name or email ✕ Route or travel history ✕ Third-party sharing ✕ Analytics or ads ✕ External servers

The app has no backend. No data leaves your phone.

Background location

The app requests "Allow all the time" location permission so departure reminders can fire even when the app is closed. This permission is used exclusively for geofence exit detection — never for tracking, advertising, or profiling.

Location is accessed for a few seconds per departure event, then stops immediately.

Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time without contacting us.

1
Uninstall the app

Removes all data immediately and permanently.

2
Clear app data

Settings → Apps → Remigo → Storage → Clear data. Keeps the app installed but wipes all places, items, and settings.

There is no account or server-side data — nothing to request deletion of from us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The updated version will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
